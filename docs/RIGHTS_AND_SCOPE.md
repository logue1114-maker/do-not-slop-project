# Rights, provenance and scope

The included research synthesis, operational rules, synthetic example content, code and comparison boards were authored for this project. External sources are cited by links, bibliographic metadata, bounded paraphrases and limited short quotations. Authorship/provenance review is not a legal ownership certification.

No reuse license has been selected. Public source visibility is not an MIT, Creative Commons or other downstream licensing grant. External source rights remain unknown unless specific permission/license evidence is established.

Third-party article bodies, product screenshots, downloaded images/GIFs/video, contact sheets, source-font binaries and uncleared generated Site art are omitted. The included SVGs contain authored vector/text shapes without embedded product screenshots; font-family names refer to installed/system fallbacks, not bundled font files. PNGs are renders of those authored synthetic boards.

Before/after examples use fixed synthetic data and shared behavior. Their dimensions, copy and layout are proposals, not source-product measurements or demonstrated usability improvement. Historical articles, creator explanations and still images do not establish a current product's behavior.

Code/contract tests are distinct from browser rendering, keyboard/accessibility, host loading, model activation, user approval and participant research. The plugin remains draft.3 with those gates unestablished.

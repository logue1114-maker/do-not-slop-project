Official button examples

Design a combination of surface, text, border, action hierarchy and interaction states. Recognizable brands use black, purple, green and blue primary actions. Brand color need not equal primary-action color, and distinctiveness also comes from typography, shape, spacing and surrounding content.

Shopify

Polaris App Home web-component examples on white

The current official preview shows a near-black filled primary button with white text, a white secondary button with dark text and a thin border, and an unfilled tertiary button. The green Shopify logo does not determine this button palette.

What this shows: A neutral action family can have clear hierarchy without a saturated brand-colored primary button.

Rule and limits

Primary is high emphasis and should be used sparingly; secondary is medium emphasis; tertiary is low emphasis. Critical tone is reserved for destructive actions.

Slack

Current Korean marketing homepage on white

The primary Get started action uses purple #730394 with white text. Find your plan uses white fill with purple #730394 text and border. Both observed buttons have 4px corner radius. Large black typography and white space form the surrounding context.

What this shows: One accent can form a family of filled and outlined actions while the rest of the page remains largely neutral.

Rule and limits

No global Slack button rule is inferred from this marketing page. These are observed rendered styles, not a published permanent token specification.

Observed 2026-10-04. Marketing campaigns, locale and product interfaces may differ. No numbered version is published for this page.

GitHub

Primer Product UI, current light theme

Published light-theme tokens specify primary green #1F883D with white text, hover #1C8139 and active #197935. Default buttons use #F6F8FA, text #25292E and border #D1D9E0; their hover fill is #EFF2F5 and active fill is #E6EAEF.

What this shows: Primary green, neutral supporting actions and blue interaction cues can coexist because they have different roles.

Rule and limits

Success roles include primary buttons and positive states; accent roles include links, selection and focus; danger roles include destructive actions and errors. Use the highest-priority button sparingly, with no more than one in a button group.

Checked 2026-10-04. These documentation values are the active light theme; other themes differ. The page is current documentation rather than a pinned package version.

IBM

Carbon White theme

Published tokens specify primary #0F62FE with white text, hover #0050E6 and active #002D9C. Secondary is charcoal #393939 with white text and hover #474747. Tertiary uses blue text and border with transparent fill.

What this shows: Even a familiar blue action becomes a deliberate system when paired with charcoal, outlines and distinct interaction states.

Rule and limits

Button variants and their hover, active, focus and disabled appearances use role-specific tokens. Focus combines #0F62FE with an inset white contrast border; disabled button fill is #C6C6C6. Typography, dimensions and spacing are also specified.

Checked 2026-10-04. The button specification states Last updated Sep 30, 2026. These values apply to the White theme and should not be copied indiscriminately into other themes.

GOV.UK

Current button example and functional colors on white

The current Save and continue example has green #0F7A52 fill, white text and square corners. Functional colors include white body background, #0B0C0C body text, #484949 secondary text, #CECECE border and #1A65A6 links.

What this shows: Green actions, blue links and yellow keyboard focus can be separated by function, supported by strong text and geometry.

Rule and limits

Use functional color tokens for their intended purposes. Yellow #FFDD00 is for focus indication, paired with dark focus text; it is not a general decorative accent. Avoid multiple main calls to action because this weakens their impact and makes the next step less clear.

Checked 2026-10-04. Button colors were refreshed in GOV.UK Frontend v6.0.0 in Feb 2026; the page also records v6.4.0 changes in Jul 2026. Older green #00703C is not the observed current default.

Spotify

Current signup screen; historical official design rationale

The current signup screen uses a bright green pill-shaped Next action with black text on a dark page. Social signup actions use white text, dark fill and gray outlines. This is a dark-surface contrast case, not a white-surface template.

What this shows: A bright fill does not automatically call for white text. Consider foreground contrast, shape and label treatment together, and adapt the combination to its actual surface.

Rule and limits

Spotify Design's 2021 article explains the choice of black foreground on Spotify green to improve contrast while retaining brand character. It also describes sentence-case labels and reduced padding. This historical explanation is not a specification for every current Spotify button.

Signup screen observed 2026-10-04. Locale and live interface may differ. The official design rationale was published 2021-06-01 and is explicitly historical.

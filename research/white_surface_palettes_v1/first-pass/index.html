    Review checklist & sources
    • Compare both colors using the same open editor, input, completion state, delivery choice, or lesson position
    • Keep every canvas and main surface white. Accent surfaces belong only to small current-state indicators
    • Use one filled primary action, an outlined secondary action, and a quiet text utility. Never let status color replace a written label
    • Verify hover, invalid input, success text, and keyboard focus. Body text is 16px; controls and labels are at least 14px
    • Check 390px and 1165px widths, no horizontal overflow, keyboard reachability, and content-driven heights
    • Numeric contrast checks are recorded separately. They are not a full WCAG certification or rendered browser test

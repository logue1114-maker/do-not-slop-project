# CP01 trial 01: blinded artifact-review findings

An independent AI artifact reviewer inspected anonymized X/Y source and screenshots with the fixed rubric. Its scores were frozen before the arm mapping was revealed. Presentation order was X, then Y; the labels were randomized and the mapping was recorded. After reveal: X = A / ordinary request; Y = B / project-guided request. Neither design has user approval.

## Descriptive findings in this pair

- **A:** Before uses a white outlined Find button; After uses a filled green button for the same supported action. This gives one copy specimen greater action emphasis. A keeps its local educational-demo label visible near the top and is more compact vertically
- **B:** Both specimens use the same filled green Find button and neutral framing. Narrow modification values sit beneath the document names, keeping the relationship readable while adding vertical length. Its shared-facts disclosure and educational-demo boundary are below the initial desktop fold; more scrolling is needed
- **Both:** The protected authored titles/body, two document records/order/values, demo-only action and attribution were retained. Both still use green actions and neutral backgrounds. The initial narrow viewport does not show both complete specimens and actions simultaneously

The one-point difference concerns equal emphasis for equivalent actions. These subtotals are not a validated overall ranking or winner, and they are not a usability measure. Both interaction scores are capped because full keyboard traversal/order is unrun; a demonstrated defect is not inferred from that missing check.

## Evidence and limits

The reviewer checked screenshots and source, and relied on the supplied browser spotchecks for action/repeat/reset/disclosure, keyboard-button activation, focus and overflow. It did not independently execute browser tests. Source/screen content and boundary gates showed no visible failure; action and executable gates rely on supplied tests. The run-integrity gate was not independently verified by the blinded reviewer before scores were frozen.

Subsequent retained metadata identifies the same requested/accepted `gpt-6.1-sol` and `xhigh` settings, equal common starter/fixture/checklist and public prompt core, no inherited conversation, one first pass each, equal repair allowance, and no implementation repairs. Exact backend revision/sampling, a deterministic seed, and full shared-runtime parity remain unavailable. No fixed time budget was enforced, and duration was not controlled. Do not convert those partial facts into a fully controlled causal experiment or a verified run-integrity pass.

Native-phone, screen-reader, full keyboard-order/trap, zoom, comprehensive contrast/accessibility, human timing/task-success/workload, and preference studies remain unrun. The prior style dissatisfaction is not established as resolved. One descriptive pair cannot demonstrate general instruction effects or measured usability improvement.

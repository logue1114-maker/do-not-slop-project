ORIGINAL INTERACTIVE STUDY

Fleet command

Shared state

Either frame controls both. Click a friendly ship to select it; Shift + click adds or removes. Choose Move or Attack, choose a target, then Confirm. Stop sends immediately. M / A / S and Escape work only within a focused frame; Enter and Space keep their native button behavior.

Study boundaries and sources

A and B share one fixture and one state, including hover, focus, selection, targets, errors and committed orders. A is an authored high-footprint baseline; B is an authored compact proposal. Neither is a screenshot, reconstruction or measurement of an existing game. Commands create records and lines; ships do not simulate movement or damage.

Source-backed design inputs: Homeworld 3 official demo feedback (control conflicts, concurrent movement/attack lines, HUD scale) and Homeworld: Vast Reaches designer documentation (selection and hover states). Their actual product results do not establish this demo’s usability.
